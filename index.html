The CFM truth

Marketing measures flow at 40 PSI; tools breathe at 90. The honest figure runs about 80% of the sticker - the 5 CFM box is a 4 CFM pump.

The recovery truth

The tank holds a fixed gulp of free air between cut-in and cut-out. At the honest CFM, refilling it is a wait the tool feels every cycle.

The duty truth

Put the tool's demand next to the pump's honest flow. Under half it runs all day; over the whole number the tool starves mid-pass.

What it never does

It never sells you the bigger tank, never calls a sticker a promise, and never forgets the derate is a labeled norm - the sander keeps the honest ledger.

Compressor Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
